The mechanic that keeps the button reachable
1  Measure the real block

A ResizeObserver reports the composer's height into a CSS variable. Today a fixed 16rem is assumed. That is 256 px: more than the desktop block needs, and far less than the phone's, so the phone is where the card disappears.

2  Scroll to the button, not the end

When a draft arrives, the card's action row is what gets scrolled into view. Landing on the end of the conversation is what buries it.

3  Say so when it is out of sight

The bar appears above the composer only while an unapproved draft is out of view, and it names the draft. It is the only thing that ever sits there. See the phone artboard called Pending bar.

Kept exactly as it is

You chose correctness over comfort, so none of the rigour is softened. The refusal stays a refusal: where the consequence of a change cannot be computed, the card says so rather than offering a friendlier estimate. Every boundary sentence and the privacy note survive word for word; they are restructured and made legible, not trimmed. The engine's rule that an unknown is never treated as zero is untouched.

Two copy bugs are fixed rather than preserved: the hardcoded "Will not:" prefix in front of an already-complete sentence with its doubled period, and a row whose before and after are both "unknown" rendering as a struck-through unknown followed by unknown.
