True of both, and worth knowing before you pick

The objects are drawn, not rendered. The published page cannot fetch images, so the seal and the document are inline vector art with gradients. They will read as illustration rather than photoreal 3D, which I think is the right register for money anyway.

Fonts come from Google Fonts. That is the one network exception the page gets. PNG and PDF export will fall back to the metric-matched stack, so exported frames will not look exactly like the screen.

Only the assistant surface is designed here. Activity's ledger, Documents and the settings page are the surfaces most likely to resist this much material. I would test the chosen direction against the ledger before committing the rest of the app.
